## 3. The man

### 3.1 Education and career (his own words, two tellings — printed unmerged)

**Telling one** — the bio on his "Humane Arts" podcast (mirrored at eden.co.uk):
"I am a student of language and literature. I have been reading, writing, and studying for
most of my life. I received a B.A. in English from Fresno State and then went on to receive
an M.A. and Ph.D. in English at Indiana University. I am interested in literature,
philosophy, history and gardening. For the last 16 years, I have been a teacher at
Peninsula College and, when not working, reading, or writing, spend much of my time in my
garden." The listing adds: "By Wesley Cecil — 135 episodes available."

**Telling two** — the bio on *The Wes Cecil Podcast* (Listen Notes): "My lectures are
dedicated to making Philosophy in particular and the world of ideas in general available
to everyone… I have PhD in Literature and Philosophy and was a college professor for over
20 years. I am working to remove the barriers that prevent many from experiencing and
understanding the lives and thoughts of some of the world's greatest thinkers."

**Disagreements, unmerged:** Telling one names the degrees as English degrees (BA Fresno
State; MA, PhD Indiana University) and gives 16 years **at Peninsula College**; Telling
two names the doctorate "in Literature and Philosophy" and gives **over 20 years** as a
college professor, in the past tense ("was a college professor"). The two are from
different podcasts of different years and may both be true at their dates (16 years at one
college within a 20-plus-year career); the record is printed as found, not harmonized.

**Career facts that stand on both tellings and the college's own pages:** he taught at
Peninsula College in Port Angeles, WA — English/humanities (the college's English Division
page describes the division he taught in; his lectures were delivered at the college, per
nearly every video description). His lecture series at the college include the Studium
Generale-style public lectures (the college catalog describes Studium Generale as public
Thursday lectures in the Little Theater across the humanities).

**Present chapter — France.** His Patreon (patreon.com/WesCecil, fetched 2026-10-20) is
titled "Wes Cecil — An Invitation to Philosophy and the World of Ideas" and says: "I'm
working to create an alternate path for those seeking intellectual enrichment and an
introduction into the world of philosophy. I'll be posting regular lectures on a range of
subjects as well as exclusive updates on the progress of creating a school of sorts for the
curious and creative here in the heart of the Dordogne, France." His website
(wescecil.com, fetched 2026-10-20) invites readers to "join Wes, award winning teacher and
philosopher, on a journey of inspiration and transformation" at a three-day seminar &
retreat at a 16th-century manoir, "a unique approach, developed over the last decade, to
help you move from reading philosophy to a Philosophical life." Podcast listings add that
Patreon members ($2/month) receive "Wes's weekly diaries from France." No source found
states a retirement date or the date of the move; the past tense of Telling two (2024) is
the only marker, and it is printed as such.

### 3.2 Why the lectures are free (his stated philosophy, in his words)

- From wescecil.com: "This website is a response to many requests I have received
  concerning my lectures. I hope to organize my disparate work from the last twenty years
  in a way that makes them easy to access. I will also be adding new lectures, classes and
  other material related to my work. I hope this site makes my lectures and other works
  available to more people and provides them with inspiration and joy." — Wes Cecil, PhD
- From the podcast bio: the lectures are "dedicated to making Philosophy in particular and
  the world of ideas in general available to everyone," "designed to provide a foundation
  for listeners to engage in further reading and thought and develop their own conceptions
  of the topics I introduce," by a man "working to remove the barriers that prevent many
  from experiencing and understanding the lives and thoughts of some of the world's
  greatest thinkers."
- The lectures themselves are free on YouTube; the paid layer is only the $2/month Patreon
  (Q&A answers, course materials, reading lists, bonus lectures, France diaries — per
  iHeart/Acast episode notes).

**Outside praise on the record:** Numéro Cinq Magazine, quoted on his site: "Wes Cecil
is, as I have said before, a remarkable, funny, passionate lecturer, a massively helpful
Virgil in the Land of the Philosophical Shades." The magazine hosted his Wittgenstein
lecture in 2013 with its own praise: "Wes Cecil is a great undergraduate teacher, an

## 4. Method note — transcripts

The Mahabharata watch page, fetched as public text, exposes only title, description, and
keywords — YouTube does not print a transcript or a captions flag in that text. However,
captions for the video demonstrably exist: independent scrapes of that same watch page
(tkfoundation.bs and camararioclaro.sp.gov.br, which mirror YouTube pages) reproduce a
continuous English transcript of the lecture, including YouTube's own auto-generated
chapter headings — "Background / the Mahabharata / Timeline of the Iliad / Vyasa /
Dharma / Indra King of the Gods / The Tree of the Kuru Dynasty" — followed by the spoken
text itself ("This Is What the Pandava Brothers Would Do by the Way this Is How They Ended
Up with Having Five Brothers and One Wife because They Early on They Swore that They Would
Not Fight amongst Themselves for the Kingdom…"). Chapter markers of that kind are produced
from a processed caption track. Third-party transcript services likewise carry full
transcripts of other Cecil lectures (e.g., ytscribe.com for "The Ancients: Confucius").
**Conclusion, at exact width:** the Mahabharata lecture carries a YouTube caption track
(auto-generated, on the scrape evidence), and a transcript is therefore obtainable through
YouTube's own transcript panel in a signed-in browser session — the road already named to
Robert. No login, download, or workaround was attempted in this scout, per the brief.
