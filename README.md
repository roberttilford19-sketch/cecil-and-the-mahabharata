# Cecil and the Mahabharata

Robert Tilford's studies and digests of one lecture: **"The Ancients: Vyasa and The Mahabharata"** by Dr. Wesley (Wes) Cecil of Peninsula College, delivered 19 January 2018 and published on the Wes Cecil YouTube channel.

This is shelf three of the Cecil shelves:

- Shelf one — [wes-cecil-studies](https://github.com/roberttilford19-sketch/wes-cecil-studies): the channel census and the founding study package.
- Shelf two — [the-ancients-with-wes-cecil](https://github.com/roberttilford19-sketch/the-ancients-with-wes-cecil): studies of the whole "Ancients" lecture series.
- Shelf three — this shelf: the Mahabharata lecture, studied in depth.

## The Cecil Rule

> Nothing implied, nothing inferred, everything in evidence. No wholesale transcripts — Wes Cecil's lectures remain his; these shelves hold Robert Tilford's studies and digests.

Every claim in these files is tied to the lecture's own movements and timestamps, or to a named source. Where the epic's text and the lecture's telling part company, both are printed and the fork is named, never merged. Quotations from the lecture are kept short, credited, and pinned to their timestamps; the lecture itself belongs to its teacher, and every reader is pointed back to him.

## What is here

- **CECIL-CENSUS.md** — a census of the Wes Cecil lecture channel compiled from public sources (2026-10-20): coverage, counts, and what could and could not be verified, with disagreements between sources printed unmerged.
- **CECIL-MAHABHARATA-RECORD.md** — the verified record of the Mahabharata lecture itself: video facts, chapter boundaries, and the desk's digests of the lecture's movements as Robert served them, with tier notes where the lecture compresses the scholarship. The lecture's full transcript is *not* in this file or anywhere on this shelf; it serves Robert's private study and quotation-checking only.
- **VISION-AND-SLATE.md** — the study lane's vision and slate, in Robert's words arranged for his edit pass: the three works unbraided (the Mahabharata, the Bhagavad Gita, the Bhagavata Purana), the honest obstacle of scale, and Cecil's own closing questions as relayed by Robert.
- **deep-dive/** — fifteen study essays (Q01–Q15) answering the questions Cecil poses in and around the lecture, each built as a FOR case and an AGAINST case under the Cecil Rule, with a comparison at the end.

## Held back, on the record

One built file is **not** on this shelf: the served-movement note on Cecil's Taoism passage (served 2026-10-22). Its digest half is house work, but the file also carries the passage's full verbatim text, and verbatim lecture text does not go on a public shelf in this house. A digest-only version may join this shelf on Robert's word.
